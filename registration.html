Scholarships

A limited number of scholarships will be offered to participants of the 12th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics.

At least one scholarship will be awarded prior to the Summer School based on academic merit and the overall quality of the application. In addition, at least one further scholarship will be awarded after the completion of the Summer School, taking into account participants’ academic engagement and contribution during the program.

To apply for a scholarship for the 12th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics, please use the following link: https://form.jotform.com/263265430070045

Deadline: Submit your materials by 20 April 2027. You will be notified about the results about 10 days after the application.

Best Presentation / Poster Award

The 12th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics will present a Best Presentation / Poster Award recognizing outstanding research presented during the program.

The award will be given to the participant whose presentation or poster demonstrates exceptional academic quality, originality, and clarity of presentation. The selection will be made by a committee of instructors and organizers.

The award aims to encourage high-quality research and active participation among early-career researchers.

Cancellations made before May 31, 2027, will be eligible for a full refund (minus administrative fees). Cancellations made after this date are non-refundable.
