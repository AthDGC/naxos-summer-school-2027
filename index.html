Why Naxos 2027

The weight of four universities

The school is organized by the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, the University of Cambridge, Arizona State University and the University of Osnabrück, within the CIVIS European University Alliance. Your official transcript confirms the credits for transfer at your home university.

Theory and computation together

Courses, workshops and master classes on historical syntax, grammaticalization, language contact, corpora and computational methods, from leading scholars in the field.

A small school, a rare setting

Five teaching days in the Ursuline School inside the Venetian Castle of Chora. You work with the faculty every day, present a poster, and earn up to 6 ECTS credits.

What you will work on

  • Computational historical linguistics and corpus methods
  • Language change theory and grammaticalization
  • Language contact and variation, including translations
  • Phylogenetic methods for language families

Open to BA, MA and PhD students and early-career researchers. A poster session and an award for the best presentation are part of the week.

Where it happens

The Ursuline School, founded in 1672, stands inside the medieval Kastro of Naxos Town, five minutes from the harbor and a short walk from the Portara. Today it is a cultural center and a venue for academic events: historic walls, modern conference rooms, and the island at your door.
