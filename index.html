Why Naxos 2027

Credits that transfer

An official transcript from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens confirms your courses, your assessed work and up to 6 ECTS credits for transfer at your home university.

Theory and computation together

Each topic is taught both through current theory and through corpora and computational tools, in courses, workshops and master classes, so that you can apply the methods to your own data.

A small school, a rare setting

Five teaching days in the Ursuline School inside the Venetian Castle of Chora. You work with the faculty every day and can present your own research at the poster session.

Four universities, one summer school

What you will work on

  • Computational historical linguistics and corpus methods
  • Language change theory and grammaticalization
  • Language contact and variation, including translations
  • Phylogenetic methods for language families

Open to BA, MA and PhD students and early-career researchers. The best presentation or poster of the week receives an award.

All topics

Where it happens

The former Ursuline School, which began operating in 1739, stands inside the medieval Kastro of Naxos Town, five minutes from the harbor and a short walk from the Portara. Today it is a cultural center and a venue for academic events: historic walls and modern conference rooms.

Venue and the island

The island

Naxos is the largest island of the Cyclades: long sandy beaches a short bus ride from Chora, clear water, mountain villages, and evenings by the harbor after the day’s classes.
