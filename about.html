12th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics

4–9 July 2027 · Naxos, Greece

The 12th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics will be held from 4 to 9 July 2027 on the island of Naxos, Greece, at the former Ursuline School in the Venetian Castle of Chora. The school is organized by the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens in collaboration with Arizona State University, the University of Osnabrück, the University of Cambridge, and the CIVIS course “Diachronic Linguistics in the 21st Century”.

The programme is designed for BA, MA, and PhD candidates. Whether you are investigating historical syntax, modeling linguistic evolution, or exploring digital corpora, the summer school offers an opportunity to engage with leading scholars and to expand your research horizons in one of the most inspiring settings in the Mediterranean.

Participants explore language evolution through theoretical courses, computational workshops, and master classes led by international experts. The school also features a workshop and poster session on recent research in diachronic and computational linguistics. Up to 6 ECTS credits are available for successful participants.

Organization

Organizers: Nikolaos Lavidas, Alexander Bergs, Elly van Gelderen, Ioanna Sitaridou.

The summer school is linked to the CIVIS Strand 1 Joint MA in Advanced Linguistics and to the CIVIS course “Diachronic Linguistics in the 21st Century”, whose online Spring School runs from 5 to 9 April 2027.

A small team of MA students from the Department of English, Division of Language-Linguistics, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, assists during the summer school.
