About the 12th Naxos Summer School

The 12th Naxos Summer School on Diachronic Linguistics will be held from 4 to 9 July 2027 on the island of Naxos, Greece, at the former Ursuline School in the Venetian Castle of Chora. The summer school is organized by the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens in collaboration with Arizona State University, the University of Osnabrück, the University of Cambridge, and the CIVIS Course “Diachronic Linguistics in the 21st Century”.

Core focus

Exploring the complexities of language change through theoretical frameworks and computational tools.

This intensive program is designed for BA, MA, and PhD candidates passionate about this core focus. Whether you are investigating historical syntax, modeling linguistic evolution, or exploring digital corpora, this summer school offers a unique opportunity to engage with leading scholars and expand your research horizons in one of the most inspiring settings in the Mediterranean.

Participants explore language evolution through a combination of theoretical courses, computational workshops, and master classes led by international experts. The summer school also features a dedicated workshop and poster session on the latest research in diachronic and computational linguistics. Up to 6 ECTS credits are available for successful participants.

The organizers

National and Kapodistrian University of Athens

Founded in 1837, the oldest university in Greece. Host and organizer.

University of Cambridge

Founded in 1209, one of the oldest universities in the world. Co-organizer of the school.

Arizona State University

Founded in 1885. Co-organizer of the school.

University of Osnabrück

Founded in 1974. Co-organizer of the school.

The school has run every summer since 2016 and is linked to the CIVIS Strand 1 Joint MA in Advanced Linguistics and to the CIVIS course “Diachronic Linguistics in the 21st Century”, whose online Spring School runs from 5 to 9 April 2027. A small team of MA students from the Department of English, Division of Language-Linguistics, National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, assists during the summer school.

The CIVIS course team

The CIVIS course “Diachronic Linguistics in the 21st Century” is coordinated with: Antonio R. Revuelta Puigdollers, Katrin Axel-Tober, Artemij Keidan, Joanna Kopaczyk, Adina Dragomirescu, Alexandru Nicolae, Ljuba Veselinova, Matthias Heinz.
