Accommodation

Participants book their own accommodation. Availability in Naxos Town is low in July because of high demand, so please book as early as possible. Contact hotels directly to make reservations and mention the Naxos Summer School for potential discounts.

Other Accommodation Options

Traditional guesthouses, rooms and apartments for rent, and youth hostels are available near the venue in Naxos Town (Chora). Many participants choose to stay in nearby beach areas such as Agios Prokopios, Agia Anna, and Agios Georgios. These areas are connected to Naxos Town by frequent bus service (KTEL) and offer beautiful beaches alongside accommodation options.

Booking tip: book early for best availability and rates during the peak summer season. Apartments and studios are available for longer stays at competitive prices.
