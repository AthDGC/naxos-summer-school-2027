Accommodation

Municipality Guesthouse (Vivlos)

Information on the free guesthouse of the Municipality of Naxos and Small Cyclades for the 2027 summer school will be added to this page as soon as the Municipality confirms its availability.

In previous years the guesthouse at Vivlos (Tripodes village, 8 km from Naxos Town, 15 minutes by bus) was available to university students (BA, MA, PhD) taking part in the summer school without a CIVIS mobility grant, on a first-come, first-served basis.

Other Accommodation Options

Traditional guesthouses, rooms and apartments for rent, and youth hostels are available near the venue in Naxos Town (Chora). You are strongly advised to do your own research as well, in order to find the best option for you. Availability can be low in July due to high demand, so we propose booking as soon as possible. Please contact hotels directly to make reservations, and mention the Naxos Summer School for potential discounts.

Many participants choose to stay in nearby beach areas such as Agios Prokopios, Agia Anna and Agios Georgios. These areas are connected to Naxos Town by frequent bus service (KTEL) and offer beautiful beaches alongside accommodation options.

Booking Tip: Book early for best availability and rates during the peak summer season (July). Apartments and studios are available for longer stays at competitive prices.
